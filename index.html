All Exercises

Push-up

Classic bodyweight exercise for upper body strength.

  1. Start in a high plank with your hands under your shoulders.
  2. Lower your chest toward the floor, bending your elbows.
  3. Push back up until your arms are straight.

Squat

Compound lower-body movement that builds leg strength.

  1. Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart and chest lifted.
  2. Bend your knees and hips to lower, keeping your back straight.
  3. Push through your feet to stand back up.

Plank

Isometric core exercise that trains trunk stability.

  1. Place your forearms on the floor with elbows under shoulders.
  2. Step your feet back and lift your body into a straight line.
  3. Tighten your core and hold without letting your hips sag.

Dumbbell Row

Pulling movement that targets the back and arms.

  1. Place one hand on a bench and hinge forward with a flat back.
  2. With a dumbbell in the other hand, pull it toward your hip.
  3. Lower the weight slowly, then repeat on the other side.

Overhead Press

Upper-body press that strengthens the shoulders.

  1. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with feet about hip-width apart.
  2. Brace your core and press the weights overhead.
  3. Lower them under control back to shoulder height.

Bicep Curl

Simple dumbbell exercise that targets the biceps.

  1. Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand, arms by your sides.
  2. Bend your elbows to curl the weights toward your shoulders.
  3. Lower the weights slowly, keeping your elbows close to your body.

Barbell Bench Press

Chest-focused barbell press performed on a flat bench.

  1. Lie on the bench and grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Lower the bar toward your mid-chest with control.
  3. Press the bar up until your arms are extended.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Angled dumbbell press that emphasizes the upper chest.

  1. Set a bench to a slight incline and hold dumbbells near your chest.
  2. Press the weights upward above your chest.
  3. Lower them slowly back to the starting position.

Cable Chest Fly

Cable exercise that brings the arms together to work the chest.

  1. Stand between cable handles with a slight bend in your elbows.
  2. Bring your hands together in front of your chest.
  3. Open your arms slowly until you feel a comfortable stretch.

Lat Pulldown

Seated cable pull that trains the lats and upper back.

  1. Sit at the machine and hold the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Pull the bar toward your upper chest while keeping your torso steady.
  3. Let the bar rise back up under control.

Seated Cable Row

Horizontal cable pull for the middle back and arms.

  1. Sit upright with knees slightly bent and hold the cable handle.
  2. Pull the handle toward your lower ribs, keeping your back tall.
  3. Extend your arms forward slowly without rounding your back.

Face Pull

Rope cable pull that trains the rear shoulders and upper back.

  1. Set a rope attachment around face height and hold both ends.
  2. Pull the rope toward your face, leading with your elbows.
  3. Extend your arms forward slowly to return.

Dumbbell Lateral Raise

Raises the arms out to the sides to target the shoulders.

  1. Stand tall with light dumbbells at your sides and elbows soft.
  2. Raise your arms out to the sides to about shoulder height.
  3. Lower the weights slowly back to your sides.

Arnold Press

Rotating dumbbell press that works the shoulder muscles.

  1. Hold dumbbells in front of your shoulders with palms facing you.
  2. Press overhead while rotating your palms to face forward.
  3. Reverse the motion as you lower the weights.

EZ-Bar Curl

Curl an EZ-bar toward your shoulders to train the biceps.

  1. Stand tall and hold the EZ-bar with a comfortable underhand grip.
  2. Curl the bar toward your shoulders, keeping your elbows near your sides.
  3. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are extended.

Hammer Curl

Neutral-grip dumbbell curl that works the biceps and forearms.

  1. Stand with dumbbells at your sides and palms facing inward.
  2. Curl the weights toward your shoulders without rotating your wrists.
  3. Lower them slowly back to your sides.

Triceps Pushdown

Push a cable handle down to strengthen the triceps.

  1. Stand at a cable machine and hold the attachment near chest height.
  2. Keep elbows by your sides and press the handle down.
  3. Bend your elbows slowly to return to the start.

Overhead Triceps Extension

Extend a weight overhead to work the back of the arms.

  1. Hold one dumbbell overhead with both hands and elbows pointing upward.
  2. Bend your elbows to lower the weight behind your head.
  3. Straighten your arms to raise it overhead again.

Hack Squat

Machine squat that trains the quads, glutes, and other leg muscles.

  1. Set the machine to a comfortable load and place your shoulders under the pads.
  2. Lower by bending your knees while keeping your back against the support.
  3. Push through your feet to return to the start without locking your knees.

Leg Press

Machine exercise for building strength through the legs.

  1. Sit against the pad and place your feet about shoulder-width on the platform.
  2. Lower the platform until your knees bend comfortably.
  3. Press through your feet to extend your legs without locking your knees.

Romanian Deadlift

Hip-hinge movement that targets the hamstrings and glutes.

  1. Stand holding a barbell or dumbbells in front of your thighs.
  2. Push your hips back and lower the weight along your legs with a flat back.
  3. Drive your hips forward to stand tall again.

Leg Curl

Machine curl that isolates the hamstrings.

  1. Adjust the machine so its pad rests comfortably above your heels.
  2. Bend your knees to curl the pad toward the backs of your thighs.
  3. Return to the start slowly and with control.

Leg Extension

Machine exercise that isolates the quadriceps.

  1. Adjust the seat so your knees line up with the machine pivot.
  2. Extend your knees to raise the pad in front of you.
  3. Lower the pad slowly to the starting position.

Standing Calf Raise

Raise your heels against resistance to train the calves.

  1. Stand with the balls of your feet on the platform and heels lowered.
  2. Rise onto your toes as high as is comfortable.
  3. Lower your heels slowly for a controlled stretch.

Hip Thrust

Hip extension exercise focused on the glutes.

  1. Sit with your upper back supported by a bench and feet flat on the floor.
  2. Drive through your feet to lift your hips until your torso is level.
  3. Lower your hips under control and repeat.

Dead Bug

Controlled floor exercise that builds core stability.

  1. Lie on your back with arms raised and knees bent above your hips.
  2. Extend one arm and the opposite leg while keeping your back gently down.
  3. Return to the start and alternate sides with control.

Hanging Knee Raise

Raise the knees while hanging to train the abdominal muscles.

  1. Hang from a stable bar with your shoulders engaged.
  2. Lift your knees toward your torso without swinging.
  3. Lower your legs slowly back to the hanging position.

Russian Twist

Seated trunk rotation that challenges the obliques and core.

  1. Sit with knees bent, feet on the floor, and torso slightly leaned back.
  2. Rotate your torso gently from side to side while keeping your chest lifted.
  3. Move under control and avoid twisting through your lower back.
